## 📌 Overview

This project investigates how **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) monetary policy decisions** — particularly changes in the **Repo Rate, Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR), and Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR)** — influence the stock returns of major sectors listed on the **National Stock Exchange (NSE) of India**.

By applying statistical methods, this study aims to understand the **transmission mechanism** of monetary policy into equity markets at a sectoral level.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- To examine the relationship between RBI policy rate changes and sectoral stock returns
- To identify which sectors are most sensitive to monetary policy announcements
- To analyze return behavior before and after RBI policy announcements

---

---

### Statistical Methods Used
- **Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) Test** — to check stationarity of return series
- **Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Regression** — to estimate the impact of policy rate on returns
- **Pearson Correlation Analysis** — to identify sector-wise relationships
- **Event Study Methodology** — to analyze abnormal returns around policy announcement dates
- **Descriptive Statistics** — mean, variance, skewness, kurtosis of returns

---

## 📋 Key Findings

- **Real estate** sectors show the highest sensitivity to repo rate changes
- **IT and Pharma** sectors are relatively less affected by domestic monetary policy
- A **rate cut** generally leads to positive abnormal returns in rate-sensitive sectors
- **Short-term market reaction** is stronger than long-term impact across all sectors
